Rzk.TypeCheck.MetaPrefix

Description

Diagnostics for unsaturated schematic parameters.

A declaration's meta prefix ends at its last schematic parameter: a universe, CUBE, TOPE, or a function quantifying over or into one.

  • Structural checking requires the full prefix at object-level uses.
  • Strict checking confines schema arguments to a top-level receiver's meta prefix.

Strict checking is the default. Neither check establishes universe levels; the Hurkens construction still passes these checks.
